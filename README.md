# Do, Re, Keys 

DoReKeys does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Microphone audio is processed locally in real time on your device Solely to detect musical pitch during practice and is never recorded or sent off-device.


App Purpose, Target Audience, Problem Solved, and Value Provided
- Purpose & Target Audience: DoReKeys is an educational music theory and piano sight-reading trainer built for absolute beginners, music students, children, and self-taught piano learners.
- Problem Solved: Beginners often struggle to connect written musical notes on the Treble and Bass staff to physical keys on a piano, and existing apps frequently require subscriptions, accounts, or internet connectivity.
- Value Provided: DoReKeys provides an immediate, distraction-free, 100% offline learning environment combining:
  (a) An interactive sight-reading trainer with progressive difficulty levels (Beginner 5-note range up to Ledger Lines and Sharps/Flats), supporting both standard Letter names (C, D, E) and Solfège (Do, Re, Mi).
  (b) Real-time on-device microphone pitch detection so learners can practice on a real acoustic or digital piano and receive instant visual feedback, alongside a built-in polyphonic touch keyboard for practicing on the go.
  (c) An interactive Music Theory guide with playable audio examples (Staff & Clefs, Note Values & Rhythm, Time Signatures, Scales & Intervals, and Chords).
  (d) A visual pendulum metronome with customizable tempo, time signatures, and beat subdivisions.
3. Instructions for Setting Up and Accessing Main Features
- No login credentials, accounts, external hardware, or sample files are required. All features are immediately accessible upon launching the app:
  • Practice Tab (Default screen): Displays a musical staff and an interactive piano keyboard. Tap keys on the on-screen keyboard to match the displayed note on the staff. Use the top toolbar controls to switch Clef (Treble, Bass, Grand Staff), Difficulty Level (Beginner, Easy, Medium, Advanced), Notation (C D E vs. Do Re Mi), Hints, or tap "Mic" to enable real-time microphone pitch detection from a physical piano. Tap the bar-chart icon to view per-note accuracy and response-time mastery statistics.
  • Learn Tab: Tap "Learn" in the bottom tab bar to browse five interactive music theory chapters with playable audio examples and rhythm/scale demonstrations.
  • Metronome Tab: Tap "Metronome" in the bottom tab bar and tap Play to start the visual pendulum metronome, adjust BPM, tap tempo, or change time signatures and subdivisions.
4. External Services, Tools, or Platforms Used
- None. DoReKeys is 100% self-contained and runs entirely offline on-device using native Apple frameworks only (SwiftUI, AVFoundation/AVAudioEngine for real-time audio synthesis and microphone capture, and Accelerate/vDSP for on-device pitch detection). It uses no external APIs, third-party SDKs, analytics, authentication services, payment processors, or AI services.
5. Regional Differences
- None. DoReKeys functions identically across all countries and regions worldwide, and includes built-in support for both Letter notation (C, D, E) and Solfège notation (Do, Re, Mi) for international music students.
6. Regulated Industry / Protected Third-Party Material
- Not applicable. DoReKeys does not operate in a regulated industry and contains no third-party copyrighted or licensed content. All code, synthesized audio, and educational materials are original.
